Design and build the storefront for **Canopy**, an online shop that sells umbrellas: compact travel umbrellas, large golf umbrellas, clear dome umbrellas, kids' umbrellas and accessories (sleeves, stands). About 12 products, priced $19 to $149.

Build four linked pages:

1. `index.html`: home page. Brand and value proposition, featured products, the collections, a reason to trust the shop (reviews, guarantee or shipping terms), and a footer.
2. `products.html`: product list. Filter or sort controls, a grid of product cards with name, price, rating and an image.
3. `product.html`: one product (pick a hero product). Gallery, name, price, colour and size options, quantity, add-to-cart, description, specs, and reviews.
4. `cart.html`: a cart with 2-3 items. Line items with quantity controls, a summary with subtotal, shipping and total, a promo code field, and a checkout button.

The same header (logo, navigation, search, cart with item count) and footer are on every page. Every page must work at desktop width and on a phone.
